Friedrich Nietzsche (1844-1900), son of a Lutheran pastor from Röcken, in Prussian Saxony, a prodigiously gifted philologist appointed professor at Basel at twenty-four, the friend and then the adversary of Wagner, ill, pensioned off from 1879, wandering between the Engadine, Nice and Turin, sinks into madness in January 1889 and survives eleven years in the care of his mother and then of his sister, who will fabricate *The Will to Power*. His work, aphoristic and polemical, called morality, truth and religion into question at their root.

#### From tragedy to genealogy

*The Birth of Tragedy* (1872) opposes in Greek art the Apollonian, form and measure, to the Dionysian, intoxication and dissolution, and accuses Socrates and rationalism of having killed tragedy; the book ruins his career as a philologist. The *Untimely Meditations* (1873-1876) attack Strauss and history, and celebrate Schopenhauer and Wagner. The so-called positivist period, *Human, All Too Human* (1878), *Daybreak* (1881), *The Gay Science* (1882), practises a demystifying psychology of ideals; *The Gay Science* announces the death of God through the mouth of the madman and introduces amor fati. *Thus Spoke Zarathustra* (1883-1885), a prose poem, preaches the overman, the meaning of the earth, the death of God, the will to power and the eternal return of the same, an abysmal thought which demands that one will each instant as if it were to return eternally. *Beyond Good and Evil* (1886) and *On the Genealogy of Morality* (1887) analyse the origin of values: the morality of masters, which says good and bad, was overturned by the morality of slaves, which says good and evil out of ressentiment; bad conscience is born of instinct turned against oneself; the ascetic ideal gives a meaning to suffering, and science is its last form. The writings of 1888, *Twilight of the Idols*, *The Antichrist*, *Ecce Homo*, radicalize the attack on Christianity, a religion of pity and ressentiment, invented by Paul, and oppose Dionysus to the Crucified.

#### The concepts

The will to power is not the thirst for domination but the ground of every living thing, which wants to grow, to interpret, to impose forms on itself; truth is an interpretation useful to a type of life, and science still rests on a faith. Nihilism is the state in which the highest values devalue themselves, a consequence of the death of God, which Nietzsche wants to pass through by a revaluation of all values. The overman is the man who creates his values without a world behind the world; the eternal return, the ordeal that selects him; amor fati, his attitude towards fate. Nietzsche refuses to be a founder of religion and wants no believers.

#### The readings

Falsified by his sister and annexed by Nazism, Nietzsche was restored by the Colli-Montinari edition. Heidegger makes him the last metaphysician, who completes Platonism by overturning it; Löwith, Jaspers, Bataille, Deleuze, Foucault and Girard have proposed opposing readings of him; Camus sees in amor fati the moment when revolt turns into total consent.

#### What the essay assumes

The essay assumes familiarity with § 108, 125, 276 and 344 of *The Gay Science*, the third essay of the *Genealogy*, *The Antichrist* on Jesus and Paul, the overman, the eternal return and Heidegger's readings. It makes Nietzsche an exception of attitude, unstable, who escapes Stirner's law because he knows where his fire comes from, and reports his attested responses to Schopenhauer, Strauss, Kant and Renan, as well as his constructed confrontations with Dostoevsky.
